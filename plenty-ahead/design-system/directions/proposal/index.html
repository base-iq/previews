The contract

What this changes in the design system

On approval, these entries are written to brain/brands/plenty-ahead/design-system.md (and the two voice devices noted to the writing guide). Everything else in the locked system stands.

Lora Italic New
Added to the type roster as the editor's voice: dateline/edition number, chapter headings ("In this edition"), the Worth-your-time label, and the sign-off. Never used for body copy, headlines, or UI controls.
Numbered entry New
The Daily's item unit: Lora 700 24px coral numeral, hanging left. Replaces the plain story item inside numbered editions; the un-numbered story item remains for site and non-almanac surfaces. Falls within coral's existing "sparing editorial accent" allowance.
Scam alert Revised
Now composable as a numbered entry: Soft Peach fill and 4px coral border-left wrap the entry in place, keeping the alert inside the reading flow instead of interrupting it. Required content order unchanged.
Edition masthead Revised
Centered stack: italic dateline + edition number, wordmark, italic brand promise, horizon mark. Edition numbering (No. 214) becomes a permanent masthead element.
The sky New
A mandatory 96px (72px mobile) of empty space between masthead and first content in every edition and page header. No content, no decoration. This is Direction A's surviving contribution.
Horizon mark usage Revised
Canonical placement: opens the edition (in the masthead) and closes it (above the sign-off). The "at most twice" rule becomes exactly these two, plus never elsewhere in an edition.
Edition sign-off New
Closing horizon mark + italic signed line ("More tomorrow. — The editors"). Every edition ends signed; the sign-off text may vary by edition but stays under eight words.
Sunrise architecture New
The large flat sea-glass semicircle rising into an evergreen field. Permitted only on acquisition surfaces: landing pages and the welcome email hero. Never inside regular editions, never animated, never gradient-filled. Same geometry at 32px is the proposed compact symbol for avatar and favicon.
Boundary constraint New
The almanac register must not drift into broadsheet territory: no double rules, no column layouts, no all-caps serif mastheads, no newsprint tinting. Those devices belong to AmericanAdvisor.
Before this ships

Open questions

  1. Edition numbering: start at No. 1 on launch day, or seed at a higher number? Almanac logic says count honestly from 1.
  2. The sign-off voice: "— The editors" (institutional) or a named editor (personal)? Named builds more attachment but creates a staffing dependency.
  3. Welcome email: full sunrise hero (C) or a compact evergreen band? Needs a rendering test in Gmail and Outlook before it enters the spec.
  4. Logo production: does the sunrise-as-compact-symbol proposal here become the brief for the logo round, or does logo exploration stay open?
