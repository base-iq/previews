The one decision in this round

The ring–dot relationship

Gap centered at 45° (up-forward), spanning ~55° — wide enough to never read as a power button, aimed so the mark always moves up and to the right. The remaining variable is how the dot relates to the ring: held close, reaching further, or heavier. This changes the mark's emotional temperature.

B1 · Held

Dot close to the opening — within reach. Confident and warm; the "plenty" feels attainable, not aspirationally distant.

Recommended
B2 · Reaching

Smaller dot, further out — more longing, more horizon. Elegant at display size but the dot risks detaching at 16px.

B3 · Bold

Heavier stroke, bigger dot. Maximum small-size punch; slightly less air, reads a touch more corporate.

Keeping it honest

Usage

Do

Flat evergreen on light, Open White or Sea Glass on dark. Gap always aims up-and-forward (45°). Use the compensated cut below 32px.

Do

Use the symbol alone only below ~110px wide or in fixed square placements (avatar, favicon, app icon). Everywhere else, wordmark or lockup.

Don't

Rotate it. Gap-down closes the circle's story into an ending — the exact wrong metaphor. Gap-up-left points backward.

Don't

Move the dot inside the ring (notification badge), close the gap, add gradients, outlines, shadows, or set it in coral.
