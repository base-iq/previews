Plenty Ahead
Logo Exploration · Round One

The wordmark is an Approved Foundation and needs faithful production, shown first in its treatments. The compact symbol is Not Yet Designed — five candidates below explore the agreed territory (forward momentum, openness, abundance, expansion, possibility, curiosity), each tested at avatar and favicon scale, with its sunset-risk and cliché-risk stated honestly. Pick a direction to refine; nothing here touches Brain or becomes an asset until approved.

Direction chosen: B · The Open Circle — see the round-two proposal
Approved Foundation

The wordmark, produced

"Plenty Ahead" in Lora Bold, one line, tracking −0.02em (current working direction). Two canonical treatments plus the quiet panel variant. Final deliverables will be true-vector SVGs (Lora outlined to paths) plus PNG sizes — produced in the build round after the symbol direction is chosen, so wordmark and symbol ship as one consistent family.

Not Yet Designed · Exploration

Symbol candidates

All five are flat Evergreen, no gradients or effects, built to survive 16px. Each is shown at display size, reversed, as an avatar, at favicon sizes, and locked up with the wordmark.

A

The Rise

Momentum · Growth

Three dots stepping upward, left to right, each larger than the last. More, and better, as you go — momentum you can read in half a second. Warm rather than corporate because the geometry is circles, not arrows.

B

The Open Circle

Openness · Curiosity

A ring that doesn't close, with one dot sitting just beyond the opening. The circle is the life already built; the gap faces forward, and there's something past it worth going toward. The most conceptual of the five — quiet until you see it, then hard to unsee.

The Fan

Expansion · Possibility

D

Daylight

The horizon, answered

The horizon exploration, evolved to answer its own critique: the circle floats fully clear of the line. Not rising, not setting — risen. Broad daylight, the whole day ahead. Keeps the equity of the original concept while removing the twilight ambiguity.

E

The Monogram

Wordmark-derived · Safest

"PA" in Lora Bold inside the evergreen field. No metaphor, no decode — pure equity transfer from the wordmark. The pragmatic answer: it can ship immediately, works at every size, and leaves the door open to adopt a conceptual symbol later without rebranding.
