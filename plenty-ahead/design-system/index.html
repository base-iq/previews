Foundations

Governing principles

Open, not emptyGenerous space creates clarity, but every surface retains a useful editorial hierarchy.
Readable by identityStrong contrast, 17px body type, obvious links, and large targets are brand features, not accommodations.
Warmth through restraintEvergreen does the work; sea glass creates breathing room; coral marks only moments that deserve attention.
Forward motion, no theaterDirection comes from layout and the horizon motif, not sliding or pulsing elements.
Commerce is legiblePaid modules fit the system but remain unmistakably disclosed.

Motion

Standard transition: 140ms on color, border-color, and opacity. Hover states never move or scale. prefers-reduced-motion removes nonessential transitions — this page honors it.

Identity

The open horizon

A level line interrupted by a rising semicircle: possibility ahead without a literal sunset. The sun always rises from or sits above the line, never below it. Use at most twice on a page, or once in an email body plus the masthead. In editions (direction-level): exactly twice — opening the edition in the masthead and closing it above the sign-off. Never animate it, pattern it, or pair it with landscape photography.

Section divider — exhibit

The instance above is an exhibit, not page decoration. The compact symbol version (for avatars and favicons) awaits logo production; do not improvise it in prototypes.

Components

Quick take and scam alert

Quick takes carry information the reader can act on immediately: Sea Glass, one primary action maximum. Scam alerts follow a required order — recognizable signal, action to take, official verification route — with calm urgency and no fear imagery. Direction-level: inside a numbered edition, the scam alert composes as a numbered entry (see Almanac edition system below).

Scam alert
⚠ Scam alert

If your bank texts this question, do not answer it

The signal: a text claiming to be your bank's fraud department asks "Did you authorize a charge of $487.20? Reply YES or NO." Replying confirms your number is live and triggers a follow-up call from the "fraud team."

What to do: do not reply. Call the number on the back of your card and ask whether a hold exists.

Verify: real fraud alerts never ask for your PIN, password, or a code sent to your phone.

Standards

Accessibility

Age improves the design: larger type, strong contrast, generous spacing, and obvious interaction states are identity traits, not compliance work.

  • ✓Body text 17px default; 16px absolute email floor
  • ✓Touch targets at least 44×44px
  • ✓Text contrast WCAG AA minimum; body copy targets AAA
  • ✓Visible focus state on every interactive element
  • ✓Warnings use icon plus label plus text, never color alone
  • ✓Link purpose understandable out of context
  • ✓No information depends on animation
