The early signal on next year's Social Security raise

New inflation data puts the 2027 cost-of-living adjustment on track for roughly 2.6%, close to this year's 2.5%. The official figure arrives in mid-October once September inflation is counted. If the estimate holds, the average retirement check would rise about $52 a month starting in January.

⚠ Scam alert

That unpaid-toll text is not from the turnpike

The signal: a text says you owe a small toll balance, usually under $10, with a link to pay and a warning about late fees. The link leads to a card-skimming page.

What to do: delete it. Toll authorities bill by mail or through your transponder account. If you're unsure, look up your state's toll agency and check your account there.

Read the warning signs

4

Check the basement for this recalled dehumidifier

About 1.5 million dehumidifiers sold at Costco, Home Depot, and Lowe's between 2021 and 2024 were recalled Friday for overheating. The fix is a free replacement, not a repair — stop using yours and check the model number first.

Check your model number

5

Canceled flight? The cash refund is now automatic

Under federal rules now in force, airlines owe you an automatic cash refund when a flight is canceled or delayed more than three hours and you decline the rebooking. No voucher, no request form. Worth knowing before fall trips get booked.

Remember When?

Fifty summers ago, we landed on Mars

In the summer of 1976, Viking 1 sent back the first photograph ever taken from the surface of Mars — a rocky orange plain that ran on the front page of nearly every newspaper in America. The lander was built to last 90 days. It kept reporting for six years.

Live Better

The one-leg balance test is worth 10 seconds of your morning

A study in the British Journal of Sports Medicine followed adults 51 to 75 and found that being able to stand on one leg for 10 seconds tracked closely with long-term health. The encouraging part: balance responds to practice at any age. Try it near a counter, hand hovering, and switch legs.
