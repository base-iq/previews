Advertiser Account — design review

Direction B, “Your Markets” — nine clickable screens on sample data. Every screen and every special state is one tap from this page.

Before you click

  • Verify code 123456 gets you through. 000000 shows the expired-code state. Any other six digits shows the mismatch error.
  • Two demo accounts Switch from the account menu, top right (your business name, with the caret). Rivera Dental Studio — one market, Naples FL. Zia Media Group — three markets, Albuquerque / Rio Rancho / Santa Fe. The same menu has Reset demo data.
  • The clock is fixed Today is always Tuesday, September 1, 2026. The first bookable day is always Friday, September 4 — three days’ notice. Nothing here goes stale.
  • Data resets Anything you book is kept in sessionStorage (bl_advertiser) and disappears when you close the browser tab.

The flow, in order

Nine screens. Walk it top to bottom, or jump anywhere.

  1. Create account index.html Email and password, then verify in place with a six-digit code.
  2. Sign in signin.html The returning-advertiser door.
  3. Setup setup.html Two steps on one page: the business, then the markets you care about.
  4. Your markets home.html The home screen — one card per market, what runs next, what you’ve spent.
  5. Market detail market.html?m=naples Who you reach in one town, and what you have running there. Needs a market: try also Santa Fe or Albuquerque (Zia’s markets).
  6. Book a placement book.html What · where · when · pay, all on one page.
  7. Review and pay review.html Normally reached from Book — this link carries a sample order (Naples, two days, $50) so the page has something to show.
  8. Booked booked.html Normally reached by paying. Opening it directly files that sample booking into the demo account — Reset demo data clears it.
  9. Budget and receipts budget.html The monthly ceiling, the card on file, every receipt.

Special states

A link where the state has a URL. A recipe where it takes a click or two.

Create account & sign in
  • Verify your email Or reach it by submitting the form. 000000 = expired code.
  • Email already has an account Or sign up as taken@business.com.
  • Wrong email or password Or sign in with the password wrongpass.
  • Setup step 2 — pick your markets Jumps past the business form with everything blank.
Your markets
  • Never booked anything The first-run hero, audience-and-scarcity line, no money strip.
  • No markets on the account
  • Four or more markets Adds the sticky scope-pill filter bar, which doesn’t render under four.
  • Numbers unavailable Analytics fail; booking state is never blocked by it.
  • Nothing scheduled · Loading
  • Hero variants: running today · tomorrow · with the editor · later this week The “with the editor” hero is the Zia account’s default.
Market detail
  • Market full through a date Rio Rancho is sold out through Sunday Sep 6; the availability line says so.
  • Nothing running here yet Empty run sheet with a day-specific CTA.
  • Market we don’t have
  • Remove-market confirmation dialog — the only dialog in the product Recipe: open any market, scroll to the bottom, tap Remove this market.
  • Clicks module, available variant Off by design: CLICKS_AVAILABLE = false at the top of app.js, pending the click-feed fix. Every market page shows the dated-outage sentence instead. Flip the flag to see the available copy.
Booking
  • Someone took the slot while you were picking Recipe: open Book with Santa Fe selected, pick a placement type, then choose Sat Sep 5. Fires once per page load; the day is dropped and the rest of your selection survives.
  • Over your monthly ceiling Recipe: on the Rivera account (Book), pick Business spotlight, keep Naples, then tap Next 30 days. Rivera has spent $300 of a $500 ceiling, so anything past eight market-days trips the block and disables the CTA.
  • Partly available days in the calendar Recipe: switch to Zia Media Group, open Book, pick Business spotlight with all three markets. Sep 4, 5 and 6 come up partial (Rio Rancho is full, the others aren’t); Sep 9 is full everywhere. Selecting a partial day writes the per-market helper line under the calendar.
  • Book pre-loaded with three markets Same calendar without switching accounts.
  • Card declined Tap Pay once to see it; a second attempt goes through.
Budget & account
  • No charges yet · Loading receipts
  • Card on file Where the account menu’s Payment method item lands.
  • Ceiling below what you’ve already spent Recipe: Budget → Change ceiling → enter 50 → Save ceiling.
  • Account switcher Recipe: on any signed-in screen, tap the business name at the top right. The panel carries business details, payment method, sign out, both demo accounts and Reset demo data.
