04 — Worth the drive
Road trip worthy

Seven events around the state that justify a tank of gas.

🦪 Cedar Key Seafood Festival @ Cedar Key City Park | Saturday (8/29) · Cedar Key — 2.5 hrs from Orlando — Clam chowder from the people who grow the clams, 100+ art vendors, and a Gulf town that still moves at golf-cart speed. Go early; the bridge backs up by ten.
🚀 Falcon 9 Night Launch Viewing @ Kennedy Space Center | Friday (8/28) at 9:40 PM · Cape Canaveral — A night launch turns the whole sky orange, and the visitor complex keeps its viewing lawn open late. If you've only watched one from a driveway, this is the upgrade.
🎨 Mount Dora Art Stroll @ Downtown Mount Dora | Saturday (8/29) at 10:00 AM · Mount Dora — 45 min from Orlando — Gallery doors open, sidewalk easels out, and the lake breeze doing its best impression of October. Lunch at one of the porch restaurants is mandatory.
🎶 Lyle Lovett and His Large Band @ St. Augustine Amphitheatre | Sunday (8/30) at 7:00 PM · St. Augustine — One of the best outdoor rooms in the Southeast, tucked into a maritime hammock. Pair it with an afternoon on the oldest streets in America.
🐎 Grand Prix of Ocala @ World Equestrian Center | Saturday (8/29) at 6:00 PM · Ocala — Olympic-caliber show jumping under the lights, free to watch, and the people-watching in the grandstand is its own event. The on-site restaurants are better than they have any right to be.
🦋 Butterfly Rainforest Encounter @ Florida Museum of Natural History | Daily · Gainesville — A thousand free-flying butterflies in a screened rainforest, plus the new fossil hall. The kind of stop that turns a Gainesville errand into a day trip.
🎭 Hemingway Days Closing Weekend @ Key West | Friday–Sunday (8/28–30) · Key West — Look-alike contests, a mock running of the bulls, and readings in the garden of the man's own house. Peak Key West, in the best way.
